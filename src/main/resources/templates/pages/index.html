Features

Crusher is a tool that helps you track your bouldering progress, log your sessions, and crush your goals.

Track Sessions

Log your climbing sessions at different gyms and monitor your progress over time.

Rate Boulders

Rate and comment on boulders to help the community and refine your skills.

View Statistics

Analyze your climbing data with detailed statistics and insights.

See It In Action

The app is designed to be simple and easy to use, with a focus on providing a clean and intuitive user experience.
